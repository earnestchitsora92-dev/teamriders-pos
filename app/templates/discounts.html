How discounts work

1. A cashier applies a discount in the cart; the reason is mandatory.

2. The administrator either enters their password at the counter (instant approval) or approves the pending request on this page.

3. Each authorisation has a code and expires 30 minutes after approval; it can be used once. Approved-but-unused codes expire automatically.

4. Every code, reason, cashier and approving admin is stored on the sale and in this audit trail.
